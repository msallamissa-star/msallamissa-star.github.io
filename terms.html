What the app is

My World is a calm, parent-operated companion for a parent or caregiver and a young child. Version 1.1 includes Development activities, reading guidance and real animal media; Little Meals; Parent Table; and the Grown‑Ups parent area. Version 1.3 adds a parent-facing Mother Care reference and renames Grown‑Ups as Family Dashboard. The app supports family time and provides general information, not individualized medical, nutritional or developmental advice. It is not an individualized emergency assessment or triage service and does not replace emergency services. Mother Care does not assess, score, diagnose, personalise or provide branching triage. You remain responsible for decisions about care, diet and safety, and should use your own qualified professional or local emergency service when individual or urgent help is needed.

Your data

Family-entered data stays in the app’s local storage and is not automatically transmitted to My World. Browsing Mother Care saves no stage, category, topic, symptom, urgent row, pregnancy date, symptom answer, score, assessment or history. A parent can deliberately share selected content or export an encrypted backup.
