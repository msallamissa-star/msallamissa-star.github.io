My World: Mother Care, family meals and play

One calm home for a mother and her family: Mother Care, Development, Little Meals, Parent Table and Family Dashboard, in English and Arabic.

App Store availability: Apple shows the version currently available in each storefront. Version 1.3, including Mother Care and Family Dashboard, is being prepared for App Review and is not yet publicly released.

  • Mother Care. General information from pregnancy through one year after birth, with stage topics, urgent warning signs and named official-source links. Setup keeps one Mother Care answer on this device to choose the path and local reminders; browsing Mother Care saves no stage, topic, symptom, date, score, assessment or history.
  • Development. Short adult-led activities, reading guidance and real animals for calm time together.
  • Little Meals. Age-filtered meal ideas with allergens, Safe prep, recipes and shopping lists where the section is available.
  • Parent Table. Adult lunch and dinner planning, searchable recipes and shopping lists.
  • Family Dashboard. Profiles, the week, cooking history, reminders, settings, backup and restore in one place.

Privacy. Family-entered data stays in the app’s local storage and is not automatically transmitted to My World. One Mother Care setup preference stays on this device and is not sent to My World. A parent can deliberately share selected content or export an encrypted backup. On Apple devices, the app separately contacts Apple for product, entitlement, purchase and restore operations; those checks send no family-entered data to My World.

Subscription. On a fresh installation of version 1.3, a verified subscription is required after setup. Eligible Apple Accounts can confirm Apple’s 14-day introductory free trial, then the selected monthly or annual plan renews automatically unless cancelled. Ineligible accounts start the standard paid subscription when purchase is confirmed. If plans or status are unavailable, first run offers Retry and Restore Purchases only.
