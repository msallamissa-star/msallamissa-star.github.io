What the app is

My Family World is a calm, parent-operated companion for a parent or caregiver and a young child. Version 1.1 includes Development activities, reading guidance and real animal media; Little Meals; Parent Table; and the Grown‑Ups parent area. Version 1.3 adds a parent-facing Mother Care reference and renames Grown‑Ups as Family Dashboard. Version 1.6 adds a Family calendar for planning the day and the week ahead, book cover pictures that load online in the Books list, and a Spanish interface alongside English and Arabic. Plans and personal notes that the family writes are not checked by the app for age, allergy, safety or health, and a calendar entry is a plan, not a record of what was eaten or done. App recipes and activities added to the calendar are references; open them in the app and read their guidance before use. The app supports family time and provides general information, not individualized medical, nutritional or developmental advice. It is not an individualized emergency assessment or triage service and does not replace emergency services. Mother Care does not assess, score, diagnose, personalise or provide branching triage. You remain responsible for decisions about care, diet and safety, and should use your own qualified professional or local emergency service when individual or urgent help is needed.

Your data

Family-entered data stays in the app’s local storage and is not automatically transmitted to My Family World. In version 1.3, setup stores one local Mother Care preference, including whether “I’m expecting” was selected, to choose the setup path and local reminders. It is not sent to My Family World. Browsing Mother Care saves no stage, category, topic, symptom, urgent row, pregnancy date, symptom answer, score, assessment or history. A parent can deliberately share selected content or export an encrypted backup. Separately, the app contacts Apple for product, entitlement, purchase and restore operations; Apple handles the Apple Account and transaction. In version 1.6, Family calendar entries and notes are stored the same way, are included in the encrypted backup, are removed by Reset all progress, and, apart from device or iCloud backups described in the Privacy Policy, leave the device only when you choose to share a preview or export an encrypted backup. Book cover pictures in the Books list load online from Open Library and, for some covers, from Internet Archive servers. How this works is described in full in our Privacy Policy, which forms part of these terms.
