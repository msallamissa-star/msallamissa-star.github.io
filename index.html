One calm home for your family: Development, Little Meals and Parent Table now, with Mother Care and the Family Dashboard being prepared for the next update.

Available now: the App Store currently serves My World version 1.1. Next update: version 1.3 is in preparation and has not been uploaded, submitted or released. Mother Care and the Family Dashboard wording below describe that planned update, not the version currently downloaded from the App Store.

  • Development. Short parent-led activities, reading guidance and real animal media, tuned to your child's age.
  • Little Meals. Age-filtered meal ideas with allergen controls, Safe prep guidance, recipes and shopping lists.
  • Mother Care, coming in version 1.3. A no-storage reference from pregnancy through the first year, with named official-source links opened only after your tap.
  • Parent Table. A real weekly meal plan and recipe collection for the adults.
  • Family Dashboard, coming in version 1.3. The current Grown-Ups area is renamed and continues to hold your cooking history, reminders, family settings, backup controls and app preferences.
  • Private by design. No account. No ads. No third-party tracking.

Family-entered data stays in the app’s local storage and is not automatically transmitted to My World. A parent can deliberately share selected content or export an encrypted backup. On Apple devices, the app separately contacts Apple to load subscription details, check status, and process a purchase or restore; those checks send no family-entered data to My World. Family-entered data is not sold. In version 1.3, Mother Care stores no stage, topic, symptom or urgent-row selection. Feeding, activity and Mother Care guidance show the published sources that informed them.
