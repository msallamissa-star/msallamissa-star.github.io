My World Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026

Current public version: the App Store currently downloads My World version 1.1. Next update: version 1.3 is in preparation and has not been uploaded, submitted or released. Mother Care, the Family Dashboard name and every behavior labelled version 1.3 below are coming in that update and are not included in the current download. They take effect only when an installed version includes them.

My World ("the app", "we") is a calm companion for a parent and a young child: meal plans and a Development section with play ideas, age‑right reading guidance and real animal media. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with your information. It is written to be honest and complete. If anything here is unclear, contact us at the address at the end.

The short version

Family-entered data is not automatically transmitted to My World. Your children's names, age bands, cooking logs and favourites stay in the app's own storage, and animals and all media play from inside the app with no third‑party connection. You can deliberately share selected content through the iOS share sheet or export an encrypted backup file to a destination you choose. On Apple devices, the app separately contacts Apple's subscription services to load available plans, check entitlement status, and process a purchase or restore that you request. Apple handles the Apple Account and transaction under Apple's privacy terms; My World sends no family-entered data in those subscription checks. (A planned optional updates email and feature request send is off in this version; if it ever turns on, this section will say so plainly.)

What the app stores, and where

Everything below is stored only in your device's local storage, which is the app's private storage on your phone. The app does not transmit this family-entered data to My World on its own. It leaves the app only if you choose to share something yourself, such as a meal idea card, a moment, or a cooking summary, export an encrypted backup, or use one of the optional send features if a future version plainly enables it. Apple's separate subscription-service calls are described below.

  • Your setup, entered once when you first open the app: the caregiver's role, your name, each child's name and age band (you can set up more than one child), household size, a daily time goal, and the topics you care about.
  • Your child's activity in the app: which animals were opened, how many sounds or recipes were played, the number of app sessions, and simple daily counts of meals prepared and time spent in the play areas. These power the parent summary screen, called Grown‑Ups in version 1.1 and Family Dashboard in version 1.3.
  • Your preferences: the meal allergies and disliked ingredients you set, your saved favourite dishes, the chosen language, and the chosen colour theme. Little Meals settings and logs are kept separately for each child. Parent Table settings and favourites are household-wide on this device and apply whichever child profile is active.
  • Feature requests: a copy of any suggestion you write is kept on your device.

In version 1.3, Mother Care does not save which stage, category, topic, symptom or urgent row you open. It does not ask for or store pregnancy dates, symptoms, answers, scores, assessments or a maternal profile.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, device identifiers for advertising, or any persistent identifier that would identify you across apps or services.

Sharing and backups you control

My World never opens the iOS share sheet or creates a backup on its own. These actions happen only after you tap the relevant control. The share controls available depend on your app version:

  • Version 1.1: depending on the control you choose, the share preview may contain a meal idea card, a moment, a Food Story or a cooking summary. Review the iOS share preview before choosing a recipient.
  • Version 1.3 Little Meals idea: shares the meal name, preparation time and selected age band or stage; the applicable portion or, where shown, a starting-serving reference and recipe-preparation amount; responsive-feeding context; applicable allergen, serving, Safe prep, supervision and storage notes; and the ingredient list. It does not share the child's name or the cooking method.
  • Version 1.3 Parent Table idea: shares the adult-recipe label, dish name, cuisine, category, time and recipe serving size; applicable allergen, food-safety, ingredient-filter and storage notes; and the ingredient list and cooking method. It does not share the child's name.
  • Version 1.3 moment or cooking summary: shares the summary shown to you, based on the on‑device cooking log. A cooking entry records only that a recipe was cooked. It does not show what a child ate, tried, or was offered, and it does not prove allergen exposure.
  • Each version 1.3 Little Meals, Parent Table, moment and cooking-summary text share also includes the standard My World footer and public App Store download link.
  • Version 1.3 encrypted backup: creates one passphrase‑encrypted file containing the fixed backup set: family setup and profiles, progress and local usage, language and theme, Little Meals settings and per-child cooking logs, Parent Table settings and favourites, reminder settings, saved updates email and feature requests or outbox, and local session and share counters. Apple entitlement state and recovery markers are excluded. You choose where to store or transfer the file. Earlier installed versions create the encrypted backup offered by that version.

You choose the recipient or destination in the iOS share sheet. In version 1.1, after you tap a text share control, a non-cancelled native or Web Share failure may copy the text to your clipboard. Where no share API is available, the app may instead open a prefilled WhatsApp page, with clipboard copy as the final fallback. In version 1.3, a native share-sheet failure reports the problem and does not copy the text or open another destination automatically. After you send a share or backup to another person or service, that recipient or service handles the copy under its own privacy practices.

The two things you can choose to send us

In the version shipping today, sending is switched off entirely: the updates field and your feature requests are saved on your device and nothing is transmitted to us. You can still choose to share a meal idea card, a moment, or a cooking summary, or export an encrypted backup yourself, as described above; that is your action, not the app's. If a future version switches sending on, this section is the complete ceiling: the two submission types below, plus the four context values named after them, are the only data a submission would contain. Each would be sent only by your explicit action, never automatically, and this policy would say plainly that sending is on.

  1. Email updates (optional). If sending is enabled in a future version and you type your email into the updates field, in onboarding, in Family Dashboard, or on our website, we would use it to send news about new features, launch offers and app news, plus the occasional question that helps us improve the app, roughly once a month. Every email would carry an unsubscribe link. We would never sell or share the list.
  2. Feature requests. If sending is enabled in a future version, suggestion text would travel to us only when you tap Send so we could read it.

If sending is enabled in a future version, each submission would also carry four small context values to help us improve the app for families like yours: your child's age band, your device language, your device time zone region, and the app version. It would contain no names, no location beyond the time zone region, and no identifier that follows you across apps.

If enabled, submissions would be delivered into a private spreadsheet we control, processed by Google (Google Apps Script and Google Sheets) acting as our storage provider. If a send failed because you were offline, it would wait on your device and retry the next time the app opened.

Children's information

My World is operated by a parent or caregiver, not by the child. Any information about a child (name, age band, and with more than one child, each child's own Little Meals settings and cooking log) is entered by the adult and stored on the device. Parent Table settings and favourites are shared across the household on that device; they are not separate child profiles. A cooking entry records only that a recipe was cooked, not what a child ate, tried, or was offered, and not allergen exposure. My World itself does not collect or transmit that information automatically. A parent can deliberately send one of the shares or encrypted backups described above to a person or service they choose. The app is designed to avoid creating the automatic data-collection obligations that apply to online services directed to children. This policy describes the app's actual design; it is not legal advice, and parents in regulated regions remain in control of the on-device data at all times.

Media

Every photo, video, and sound in the app (the animals, the dish photos, the section images) is bundled inside the app and plays from your device. Playing it does not connect to any third party and sends nothing anywhere. A Songs area is planned but does not ship yet; its recordings stay out of the app until every licence position is fully documented.

Mother Care official-source links in version 1.3

When version 1.3 is installed, Mother Care content is bundled in the app and works offline. It makes no automatic web request and saves no Mother Care selection. A named official-source link opens only after you tap it. On Apple devices, that deliberate tap hands the exact allowlisted HTTPS page to the system browser. The official website then handles that visit under its own privacy practices; My World does not append your name, child data, Mother Care selection or another My World identifier to the link.

Daily reminders (notifications)

With your permission, the current public version 1.1 can schedule up to two daily reminders for the parent: one about the day's play idea and the little one's meals, and one about tonight's Parent Table dinner. In version 1.1, the two child-focused reminder titles can include the active child's name. Version 1.3 also lets you turn on one weekly ideas reminder. Its general body text is chosen locally from the active child's age band, so on that weekday up to three reminders can be scheduled. In version 1.3, reminder titles are neutral and do not include the child's name. All reminders are scheduled locally on your device by iOS. Nothing is sent to a server, no push service is used, and we never notify the child. You can turn each reminder off inside the app under Grown‑Ups in version 1.1 or Family Dashboard in version 1.3, or in iOS Settings for My World.

Subscriptions and payment

If you choose to subscribe, payment is handled entirely by Apple through your App Store account. We never see or store your card details. The app contacts Apple's subscription services to load available plans and check whether an entitlement is active; Apple processes those requests and any charge under Apple's own privacy terms. You can view, change, or cancel a subscription at any time in your device's App Store settings under your Apple ID.

What we do not do

  • No third‑party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No advertising of any kind.
  • No selling, renting, or developer‑initiated sharing of your information.
  • No accounts, no login, no cloud sync.
  • No push server; reminders are scheduled only on your device.

How to review or delete your data

Because family-entered data is stored locally in the app, you control the copy on this device:

  • Open Grown‑Ups in version 1.1 or Family Dashboard in version 1.3 → Settings → Reset all progress. The confirmation in your installed version tells you what it will erase. In version 1.3 this erases saved activity, cooking logs, Little Meals and household-wide Parent Table food settings, favourites, and saved or queued feature requests (a quick grown‑up check guards this). The family setup, selected language, colour theme, reminders, and any locally saved updates email remain; review every food setting before planning the next meal.
  • Or delete the app, which removes the app's local storage container from that device. Copies you deliberately shared or exported, copies held by recipients, and device or iCloud backups are outside My World's control and may remain until you delete them through the relevant app or Apple backup settings.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever adds a feature that changes how data is handled (for example an optional account or cloud backup), we will update this policy and make the change clear in the app before it takes effect.
